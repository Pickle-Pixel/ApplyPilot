# Cover Letter Template

> Fill in once Kimi K2.6 cloud is verified producing fabrication-free output. Until then, hand-edit every cover letter against this structure.

## Hard rules
- 250 words max.
- Every concrete claim must trace to `resume.txt` or `profile.json` `real_metrics`.
- Name the actual company. No "your team" / "your organization" placeholders.
- No fabricated percentages, no invented tools, no false employer history.
- No `[BRACKETED]` placeholders left in final output.

## Structure

### 1. Opening hook (2-3 sentences)
- Specific to this company and this role.
- NOT "I am writing to apply for..."
- Reference something concrete: the team's work, a product, a public stat from their site, or the exact reason this role fits.

### 2. Evidence paragraph A (3-4 sentences)
- Tie 1-2 concrete experiences from resume to a top JD requirement.
- Use JD verbatim language where it matches reality.
- Quantify only with metrics already in `real_metrics`.

### 3. Evidence paragraph B (3-4 sentences)
- Cover a second JD requirement with a different experience.
- Show range without repeating paragraph A.

### 4. Closing call-to-action (1-2 sentences)
- Specific next step ("happy to walk through X on a call").
- No "Thank you for your time and consideration" boilerplate.
