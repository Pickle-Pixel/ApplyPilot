# Resume Tailoring Framework

## Source of truth
- `.applypilot\resume-overhaul\MASTER_RESUME.docx`
- `.applypilot\resume-overhaul\` role variants
- `profile.json` for `real_metrics` and structured facts

If a claim is not in one of those, it does not go on the resume.

## Allowed tailoring moves
- Reorder bullets to surface JD-relevant ones first.
- Rewrite bullets to use JD verbatim keywords IF the keyword is genuinely in evidence.
- Drop irrelevant bullets.
- Strengthen verbs (replace weak openers with concrete action verbs already supported by the bullet).

## Forbidden
- Adding a new employer.
- Changing dates.
- Inventing metrics.
- Claiming software/tools not in `resume.txt`.
- Promoting titles beyond what `MASTER_RESUME.docx` lists.

## Pre-submit
Pair every tailored resume with the `pack_quality_gate.md` checklist. No exceptions.
